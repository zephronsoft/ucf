PROGRESS UNDER UCP LEADERSHIP

NATIONAL SECURITY

The UCP has always considered the safety and security of each and every citizen of the country with paramount importance. Under our leadership, we have seen considerable increase in expenditure on national security and public safety, ensuring comprehensive protection for all communities.

ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT

Our economic policies focus on inclusive growth, job creation, and supporting small businesses. We believe in an economy that works for everyone, not just the privileged few.

AGRICULTURAL REFORMS

Supporting our farmers with modern technology, fair pricing, and sustainable farming practices. Agriculture remains the backbone of our economy and society.

EDUCATION FOR ALL

Quality education accessible to every child regardless of their background. Building schools, providing scholarships, and ensuring no child is left behind.

HEALTHCARE SYSTEM

Universal healthcare coverage, modern medical facilities, and preventive care programs to ensure the health and well-being of all citizens.

INFRASTRUCTURE DEVELOPMENT

Building roads, bridges, digital connectivity, and modern infrastructure to connect communities and boost economic growth.

SOCIAL WELFARE

Comprehensive social security programs, poverty alleviation, and support for marginalized communities to ensure inclusive development.
