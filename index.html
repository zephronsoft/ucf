Key Issues

Social Welfare Programs

The UCF has always prioritized the welfare and development of every community member. Our comprehensive social welfare programs have seen significant expansion, with increased support for vulnerable families, emergency relief services, and community development initiatives.

  • 500+ families supported through direct assistance programs
  • Emergency relief provided during natural disasters
  • Elder care services for 150+ senior citizens
  • Community support networks established in 25+ localities
500+ Families Served

Educational Excellence

Education remains our top priority for community development. Through scholarships, skill development programs, and educational infrastructure support, we're building a more educated and empowered society.

  • 100+ scholarships awarded to deserving students
  • Adult literacy programs reaching 300+ participants
  • Skill development workshops conducted monthly
  • Educational material distribution in rural areas
100+ Scholarships Given

Healthcare for All

Universal healthcare access is fundamental to our mission. Our medical camps, health screening programs, and healthcare assistance ensure that quality medical care reaches every corner of our community.

  • Monthly medical camps in underserved areas
  • Free health screenings for 1000+ individuals
  • Medicine distribution programs
  • Health awareness campaigns
1000+ Health Screenings

Infrastructure Development

Building strong infrastructure is essential for community growth. Our projects focus on clean water access, sanitation facilities, and essential infrastructure that improves quality of life.

  • Clean water projects in 15+ villages
  • Sanitation facilities constructed
  • Community centers established
  • Road improvement initiatives
25+ Projects Completed

Environmental Conservation

Protecting our environment for future generations through tree plantation drives, renewable energy initiatives, and environmental education programs.

  • 5000+ trees planted in conservation drives
  • Solar energy projects in rural areas
  • Environmental awareness programs
  • Waste management initiatives
5000+ Trees Planted
